# CS-250: Software Development Lifecycle

## How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this?

I interpret user needs by understanding what the customer wants, asking questions when requirements are unclear, 
and breaking the requirements into smaller tasks. Creating user stories helps explain what the user needs, why the 
feature is important, and what the program should accomplish. For example, in the SNHU Travel project, a traveler might
want to compare travel options before making a decision. User stories help keep development focused on the customer and
provide a clear way to determine whether a feature meets the requirements.

## How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

I approach developing programs by breaking a larger project into smaller tasks, understanding the requirements, planning the work,
and testing the results. I learned that the Scrum-Agile approach allows a team to make changes, receive feedback, and improve the product
throughout development. In my future development work, I hope to use sprint planning, user stories, acceptance criteria, regular testing,
and sprint reviews. I also want to use retrospectives to reflect on what went well and what could be improved. These practices can help me
stay organized, manage my time, and create software that meets user needs.

## What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member means communicating clearly, listening to others, sharing ideas, completing assigned tasks, and being willing to
learn from feedback. It also means asking questions when something is unclear and helping teammates work through problems instead of placing blame.
During software development, everyone has different responsibilities, but the team shares the same goal of creating a successful product. I believe 
being dependable, open to change, and respectful of other team members helps build trust and makes the development process more successful.
